Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Short Version

We don't collect your personal data. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your rent numbers never leave your device. We don't use cookies for tracking. We don't sell data because we don't have any.

What We Collect

Nothing, really. The site uses basic analytics (page views, not personal data) to understand which tools are popular. If we ever add ads, we'll update this policy. But right now, there's nothing to track.

Local Storage

The calculator saves your last 10 calculations to your browser's local storage. This stays on your device. We can't access it. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser data.
